Modern Philosophy

Issue information, contents and author guidance for the center’s scholarly publication.

Issue 1 · George Berkeley’s Works and Legacy

Modern Philosophy is a Chinese-language scholarly publication issued in book form. The first issue brings together Berkeley scholarship, documentary and textual studies, reception history, book reviews and interviews.

Editor-in-chief: Liu Ming · Executive editor: Teng Guangwei
Fujian Education Press · May 2026 · 320 pages
ISBN 978-7-5758-1077-7
