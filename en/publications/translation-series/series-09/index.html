Sabina Spielrein: Between Freud and Jung, Volume II

萨宾娜·斯皮勒林 在弗洛伊德与荣格之间（下册）

Edited by Michel Guibal; Jacques Nobecourt. Chinese translation by 左天梦、许丹、康翀.

This English guide describes the Chinese edition. The heading is a translation of its Chinese title.

About this volume

This volume brings to light materials concerning Sabina Spielrein's study of Freud and Jung, two major figures in psychoanalysis, together with historical records of the exchanges among the three. It includes her correspondence with Freud and Jung and her personal diary, which records episodes in her relationships with both men: the making of psychoanalytic theory, love, rupture and betrayal.
