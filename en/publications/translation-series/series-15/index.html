Foucault and Madness

福柯与疯狂

By Frédéric Gros. Chinese translation by 孙聪.

This English guide describes the Chinese edition. The heading is a translation of its Chinese title.

About this volume

Madness runs throughout the development of Foucault's thought and throughout this book. Covering the early, middle and late periods of his work, this concise study traces the development of a rich body of ideas and helps readers gain a clear understanding of the subject in a short space of time. It examines the many facets of Foucault's thinking about madness, asking how institutions within a civilisation such as ours came to regard madness as mental illness, and what different meanings it held before being subjected to a restrictive medical definition.
