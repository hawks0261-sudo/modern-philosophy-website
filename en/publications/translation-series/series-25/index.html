贝克莱的世界：关于三次对话的考察

By Tom Stoneham. Chinese translation by 滕光伟.

This English guide describes the Chinese edition. The heading is a translation of its Chinese title.

About this volume

This book presents Professor Tom Stoneham's interpretation of George Berkeley's Three Dialogues between Hylas and Philonous. Published in 1713, the Three Dialogues is a major philosophical work and a crucial stage in the development of Berkeley's idealism. Through dialogue, it debates the existence of matter and the nature of perception, setting out the position usually described as immaterialism or subjective idealism. Stoneham closely examines and critically interprets Berkeley's central philosophical ideas, locating the significance of the Three Dialogues in its contribution to debates about the nature of reality, perception and knowledge. Berkeley's idealism challenged the prevailing metaphysical positions of his time. His work also influenced later philosophers, including Immanuel Kant and George Santayana, who engaged with and responded to his ideas in their own philosophies.
