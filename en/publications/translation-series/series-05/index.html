A Woman Alone

独自一个女人

By Julia Kristeva. Chinese translation by 赵靓.

This English guide describes the Chinese edition. The heading is a translation of its Chinese title.

About this volume

Centred on questions concerning women, this volume brings together essays and addresses published by the author between 1975 and 2006. Its three parts—Day by Day, Flesh and Spirit, and A Woman—draw on semiotics, psychoanalysis and anthropology, ranging across the French Enlightenment and Revolution to contemporary society. Alongside portraits of remarkable historical women such as Madame de Staël, Madame Récamier and Charlotte Corday, it concentrates on contemporary questions of fashion, culture and society affecting women. The book affirms the positive significance of women's agency and the maternal function, and advocates each woman's individual singularity and creativity.
