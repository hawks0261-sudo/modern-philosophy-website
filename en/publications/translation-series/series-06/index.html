Rebuilding Cosmopolitanism

重建世界主义

By Yves Charles Zarka. Chinese translation by 赵靓.

This English guide describes the Chinese edition. The heading is a translation of its Chinese title.

About this volume

The book is presented as an outcome of a European Union research project led by the author. It traces the philosophical and cultural origins of the word cosmopolitanism and recovers its original, positive meaning, rooted in the idea of world citizenship and the conception of the world as a city. The author's account of responsibility for humanity and the world takes debate about cosmopolitanism beyond morality into the juridical sphere. He also develops critiques of new forms of oppression and ecological problems, including the excessive exploitation of the Earth.
