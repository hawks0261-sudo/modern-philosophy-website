Bergson on French Philosophy

柏格森论法国哲学

By Henri Bergson. Chinese translation by 邓刚.

This English guide describes the Chinese edition. The heading is a translation of its Chinese title.

About this volume

This collection brings together a series of Bergson's essays and letters on French philosophy, the French spirit and French thinkers. For Bergson, the history of French philosophy since Descartes can be understood through two intersecting and mutually illuminating strands: an overt tradition of rationalism, represented by Descartes, Voltaire and Comte, and a less visible tradition centred on feeling, represented by Pascal, Rousseau and others. French philosophy has also consistently sought dialogue with science, attended to the literary qualities of philosophical expression, and aimed to speak in language that everyone can understand.
