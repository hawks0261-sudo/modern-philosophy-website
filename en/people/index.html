Our people

People

Research, translation, editorial collaboration and an international advisory network.

Director

Liu Ming · 刘铭

Liu Ming directs the Center of Modern Thought and is an assistant researcher at Yangzhou University’s Institute of Yangtze River Culture (China Grand Canal Research Institute). He coordinates academic research, international collaboration and publishing, and serves as editor-in-chief of Modern Philosophy and the Western Thought & Culture Library.

In memoriam

David Berman (1942–2026)

David Berman, formerly a professor of philosophy and an emeritus fellow of Trinity College Dublin, served as an international adviser to the center. We remember his support for scholarship on Berkeley and the history of Irish philosophy.
